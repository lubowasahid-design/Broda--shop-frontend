Music Distribution

Prepare your release for distribution to supported music platforms through BRODA HUB’s approved distribution partners.

What you should prepare

  • Your final audio master and cover artwork.
  • Artist name, release title, track titles and release date.
  • Songwriter, producer and other required credits.
  • Confirmation that you own or have permission to distribute every recording and composition.

Platform delivery is not automatic or guaranteed. Available stores, delivery times, fees and royalty reporting depend on BRODA HUB’s active partner agreements and review. Do not submit music you do not have rights to distribute.
